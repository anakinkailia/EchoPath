## Research and editing

Safari was used to browse the requested Nowcoder interview center, select the
data category, and read the Xiaohongshu collection. Safari control errors later
prevented reliable navigation; public web search and page reads supplemented
the remaining sources. No subscription-only text was accessed. The Xiaohongshu
selection uses complete questions visible before the subscription notice.

Questions are concise paraphrases, not transcripts. Generic source headings are
used when round numbers were absent. Case prompts are labeled as exercises;
missing case materials and results are not invented. Source dates refer to the
post's displayed update/publication date, not the date of every interview it
contains. The separate sample-year labels do not date these source interviews.

The source-backed sets live in `src/interview-research.ts` and are displayed only
for known fictional sample IDs. They are never written into `Experience.interview`
or submitted to the database. Actual contributor interview logs retain their
original questions, asked-back questions and outcomes, and take priority over
editorial practice material. Real posts without questions retain the empty state.
